# Recorded sounds (optional)

Drop recordings here and they replace the synthesised sounds; missing files mean synthesis.

| File | Plays | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `pour.m4a` (or `.mp3`) | during the pour, trimmed to 0.8 s with a short fade so it ends on the clink | record 1–2 s of liquid pouring into a cup |
| `coin.m4a` | as the coin lands | a short clink, under 0.5 s |
| `cheer.m4a` | with the confetti | a "woohoo!", about 1 s |

Voice Memos on an iPhone exports `.m4a`, which Safari decodes natively. Keep them short: they are precached by the service worker.
