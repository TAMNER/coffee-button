Coffee Button: three directions

The same app drawn three ways. Each direction shows the cup screen, the press ceremony on a loop, and the shelf with sample figures. Pick the one that feels right. It gets two rounds of iteration, then becomes the real artwork.

A. Margin InkB. Cut PaperC. Night Café

Direction A

Margin Ink

Everything looks drawn with a fountain pen in the margin of a notebook, with a highlighter for the money. It feels personal and a little scruffy, like a habit you are keeping track of by hand.

Direction B

Cut Paper

Flat coloured card cut into chunky shapes and stuck down with hard drop shadows, like a shop window display. It is loud and cheerful, and the numbers are set big enough to read across a room.

Direction C

Night Café

Thin pastel lines glowing softly against a late-evening sky, like a café sign seen through a rainy window. It is quiet and calm, with one warm glow when the coin lands.
