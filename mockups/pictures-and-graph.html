The latte graph, round three

Latte in a glass, as bars: dark brown coffee is what you have invested, light brown latte is saved and still waiting, and the cream froth on top marks the total saved. Steam rises off the top. Four different ideas below, each shown with one week of data and with about five months. Flip Light and Dark at the top.

Bars: with one week of data there is one bar per day (seven); with five months there is one bar per month, June to November (six, the last one is this month so far). Each bar is the running total at the end of its day or month.
