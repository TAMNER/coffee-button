The savings graph, round five

The cups are gone. Four ordinary line graphs of the same two things: what you have saved, and how much of it has been invested. Each has a money scale up the side and dates along the bottom, and each is shown with one week of data and with about five months. Touch or hover over a graph to read any day. Flip Light and Dark at the top.

Reply with one line: A, B, C or D.
